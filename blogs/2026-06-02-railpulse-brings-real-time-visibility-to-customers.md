A telematics platform is helping railroads and shippers improve visibility, performance and network efficiency across North America. RailPulse™ is advancing how the rail industry captures, shares and uses data, delivering real-time visibility into railcar location, condition and performance. Designed as a secure and standardized platform, RailPulse™ enables railroads, shippers and equipment owners to operate with greater transparency and coordination.
