At Greenbrier, our Core Value of Respect for People extends beyond the workplace and into the communities where our employees live and work. One of the ways we bring that value to life is through our ongoing partnership with Habitat for Humanity, helping create safe and affordable housing for families and individuals working toward a brighter future. Recently, Greenbrier employees in Oregon participated in another Habitat for Humanity build day in southwest Portland, volunteering their time and energy to erect walls and supports in homes that will make a lasting difference for local families.
