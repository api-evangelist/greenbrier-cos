At Greenbrier, how we build matters just as much as what we build. That mindset is at the core of our recent collaboration with SSAB Americas and Alter Trading Corporation — an initiative that brings circular economy principles to life in a practical, real-world way. Announced earlier this year, the project brings together material innovation, manufacturing expertise and end-use alignment to demonstrate how railcar design can support a more sustainable steel lifecycle.
