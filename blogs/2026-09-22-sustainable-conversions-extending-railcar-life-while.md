For railcar owners, an aging fleet does not always mean an asset has reached the end of its useful life. A railcar may have decades of service life remaining but no longer fit the needs of the market in its current configuration. As economic conditions continue to challenge companies across the United States, railcar owners are looking for ways to control costs, optimize existing assets and prepare their fleets for changing demand.
